## Changes to the Commons originals

The spring, shuttle, doge and honest-work pictures are made from the Commons originals. spring and shuttle are cropped and all four are scaled. Crop boxes are in source pixels as (left, top, right, bottom):

- spring: (361, 0, 1638, 1277), then scaled to 256 × 256.
- shuttle: (1000, 0, 3672, 2672), then scaled to 256 × 256.
- doge: not cropped; scaled so the long side is 320 px (272 × 320).
- honest-work: not cropped; scaled so the long side is 320 px (320 × 203).

The cartoon picture is drawn by `scripts/jpeg-gallery/make_cartoon.py` and is not based on any other work.
